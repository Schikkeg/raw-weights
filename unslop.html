How a video gets picked

Links are collected from recent Hacker News stories and comments, Lobsters AI/ML posts, new uploads from a small watchlist of trusted channels, and YouTube Data API searches on AI engineering topics. Each card shows where the video was first seen. Obvious non-candidates (under 8 minutes, live streams, private or deleted) are dropped before any model is involved. For each remaining video, an LLM reads the title, channel, duration, description, tags, an excerpt of the transcript when captions exist (opening, samples from the middle, and the ending), and the post that linked it. It never watches the video, so confidence is capped when there is no transcript. It applies four gates in order.

1 · Topic

Must be about building, running or understanding AI/LLM systems. Deep but off-topic content is dropped.

2 · Hype

Product announcements, tier lists, news recaps and "AI will change everything" talks are dropped.

3 · Joke check

It also reads the lines just before and after the link, and the comment being replied to. Links posted as a punchline, meme or ad are dropped, however relevant the title sounds.

4 · Depth

Needs at least one implementation signal: architecture, deployment, tool schemas, state, routing or live code. The quote that proves it is checked against the source text.

Where captions exist the model also reads a sample of the transcript (opening, middle and end); otherwise it only sees the title, description and discussion. Treat every card as a strong pointer, not a guarantee. The confidence dots on each card say how directly the context supported the pick.
