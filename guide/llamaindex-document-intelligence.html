PDF was designed for portability — same appearance on every screen, every OS. That was the win. The cost: reading order breaks when you extract text, tables fragment into noise, charts become flat images. The Citation Fallback Algorithm

Financial documents have irregular characters, currency symbols, and whitespace artifacts from PDF reflow. The algorithm degrades gracefully through four matching strategies — click each step.

1

Exact Match

Verbatim character-for-character search

Succeeds ~65% of the time

2

Whitespace Normalize

Collapse tabs, double-spaces, non-breaking spaces

Recovers ~15% more

3

Strip Currency

Remove $, £, €, ¥, commas, abbreviations

Critical for financial docs

4

Longest Subtoken

Find longest substring present — always anchors something

Last resort — never fails
