# Adobe CX Enterprise Ecosystem — Deconstructing the Agentic Web

> An independent, interactive breakdown of the 2026 Adobe CX Enterprise Architecture. Click through ten interconnected modules to see how agentic AI, Real-Time CDP, and GenStudio connect the content supply chain with customer journeys.

## Overview

This guide presents an independent analysis of Adobe's enterprise customer experience (CX) architecture as of 2026, drawing on Adobe Summit messaging, official Adobe documentation, and first-hand research. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Adobe.

Ten interactive slides walk through the full CX enterprise stack — from the AI orchestration layer at the centre through brand visibility, content supply, customer engagement, analytics, and intelligence layers. Each slide includes a "Today vs. Tomorrow" framing showing how agentic AI shifts existing workflows.

## Key Topics / Sections

- **CX Enterprise Architecture Overview**: End-to-end agentic system for Customer Experience Orchestration (CXO); the 431% ROI case for connecting Real-Time CDP, Journey Optimizer, and CJA.
- **Brand Visibility (AEM + Agentic Web)**: LLM Optimizer for AI-era search; brand truth layer in AEM; agentic authoring that lets marketers self-serve page generation.
- **Customer Engagement (AEP Native Apps)**: Omnichannel journey orchestration across email, WhatsApp, SMS, and iOS Live Activities; B2B buying group engagement with Journey Optimizer B2B Edition.
- **Content Supply Chain (Adobe GenStudio)**: 47% faster content production cycle; NVIDIA-powered 3D digital twins; Firefly and Workfront integration.
- **CX Analytics Suite**: Marketing Campaign Analytics with causal AI for MMM/MTA; Data Insights Agent as analyst-in-a-box for natural language CJA queries.
- **Adobe AI Platform & Orchestration**: Agent Orchestrator as the central reasoning engine; Agent Composer for governance; coordination of Audience, Content, and Journey agents.
- **CX Enterprise Coworker & Agents**: AI coworker that executes goal-defined workflows; MCP integration with third-party agents including Microsoft Copilot.
- **Adobe Brand Intelligence**: Living brand ontology knowledge graph; Brand Governance Agent for automated content compliance scoring.
- **Adobe Experience Platform (AEP)**: Real-Time Customer Profile, AI-powered segmentation, and the data foundation layer.
- **Engagement Intelligence**: Predictive scoring, propensity modelling, and next-best-action recommendations.
