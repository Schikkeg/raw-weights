# LLM Architecture & Lifecycle — The Full Picture

> A definitive guide mapping the journey from raw text corpora to deployed multimodal AI systems, with persona-adaptive content for Architects, Developers, QA Engineers, and Managers.

## Overview

This guide covers the complete architecture and lifecycle of a large language model — from how AI is trained on raw data through neural network mechanics, tokenisation, the Transformer architecture, frontier models, multimodal extensions, and production serving. Selecting a persona (Architect, Developer, QA Engineer, or Manager) tailors the focus of each section to the reader's specific engineering or management perspective.

Eight major sections build sequentially, giving a coherent picture of how a language model goes from a text corpus to a deployed, serving system that handles real user queries.

## Key Topics / Sections

- **How AI is Trained**: Pre-training on large text corpora; the role of compute, data scale, and loss functions; the difference between pre-training and fine-tuning.
- **Neural Network & Backpropagation**: Weights, activations, loss calculation, and the chain rule in plain terms.
- **The Big Picture: Backpropagation**: Step-by-step animated walkthrough of forward pass, loss, and gradient update.
- **Prompt Engineering**: System prompts, few-shot examples, chain-of-thought, and the limits of prompting versus fine-tuning.
- **Tokenisation**: BPE and WordPiece tokenizers; how the same text becomes different token sequences; why tokenisation matters for multilingual and code tasks.
- **Transformer Architecture**: Self-attention, multi-head attention, positional encoding, feed-forward layers, and the encoder-decoder vs. decoder-only split.
- **Frontier Models**: GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama — architectural choices and what distinguishes frontier-scale models.
- **Multimodal AI**: Vision encoders, cross-modal attention, and how image-text models combine modalities.
