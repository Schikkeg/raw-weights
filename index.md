# RawWeights — Where Learning Starts

> Exploring AI from raw fundamentals to applied intelligence. Interactive playgrounds, deep guides, and field notes on the math behind modern AI.

## Overview

RawWeights takes its name from a deliberate analogy: in a gym, raw weights mean no machines, no shortcuts — just iron, gravity, and effort. Neural network weights work the same way. They are the raw numbers a model adjusts to learn, encoding everything the model knows. This site traces that journey from the raw mathematical foundation all the way to the intelligence powering modern LLMs and agentic systems.

The site is organised around three pillars: interactive browser-based playgrounds (no Python, no setup), structured deep-dive guides covering RAG, MCP, fine-tuning, and enterprise AI, and a blog of field notes from building real systems. Everything runs in the browser and starts from first principles.

The learning path runs from simple regression (srt.html) through deep learning, transformers, RAG, agentic AI, and into production systems — a deliberate progression from fundamentals to applied intelligence.
