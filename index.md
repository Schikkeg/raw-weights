# RawWeights — Where Learning Starts

> AI from raw fundamentals to applied intelligence. Interactive playgrounds, practitioner guides, and field notes on building real systems.

## Overview

RawWeights takes its name from a deliberate analogy: in a gym, raw weights mean no machines, no shortcuts — just iron, gravity, and effort. Neural network weights work the same way. They are the raw numbers a model adjusts to learn, encoding everything the model knows. This site traces that journey from the raw mathematical foundation all the way to the intelligence powering modern LLMs and agentic systems.

The site is organised around three pillars: **8 interactive browser-based playgrounds** (no Python, no setup), **structured deep-dive guides** covering RAG, MCP, fine-tuning, AI security, and enterprise agentic systems, and a **blog and use-case library** of field notes from building real AI products.

A hero inline search bar lets visitors query across all content immediately. Topic pills offer one-click entry points into popular subjects. A tabbed "Explore" section lets visitors filter the full content library by type: All, Blog, Guides, or Use Cases — with article counts per tab.
