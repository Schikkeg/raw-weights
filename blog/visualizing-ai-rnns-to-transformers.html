Looking Under the Hood: A Visual Journey from RNNs to Transformers

Engineering & Architecture

Mar 01, 2026

We all drive cars, but very few of us truly understand the internal combustion engine. We just use the interface—the steering wheel and pedals—and trust that the machine works.

Right now, we are treating Artificial Intelligence the exact same way. Machine Learning is the complex engine, and modern AI tools are just the interface. With the explosion of new LLMs, many of us feel confused about which models to use or how to improve our prompting. We often feel that studying the underlying math is disconnected from our daily work.

But understanding the evolution of these models—like knowing if an RNN is obsolete, or how a Transformer saves processing costs—is a superpower. Once you visualize the math and interconnections, the "magic" becomes understandable engineering. Writing better prompts and reducing AI bias gets significantly easier.

Inspired by the excellent teachings of experts like Ava Amini, I have created a fully interactive, visual guide to sequence models.

The Interactive Tools

Instead of just reading math formulas, I built 13 interactive widgets you can play with to see exactly how data moves through these networks. Click any link below to jump straight to that tool in the master guide:

  • 1. The Forest Hiker
    Why does sequence matter? Toggle the history of a hiker's steps to see how a model turns wild guessing into an accurate prediction of momentum.
  • 2. AI Architecture Types
    Run math traces to see how the architecture changes whether you are doing Sentiment Analysis (Many-to-One) or Translation (Many-to-Many).
  • 3. Unrolling the Timeline
    Standard networks have amnesia. Watch an animation of how an RNN solves this by passing a "memory pad" (the hidden state) forward step-by-step.
  • 4. Rolling vs Unrolling the Graph
    Click to physically expand the traditional "loop" of an RNN into an unrolled timeline to understand how parameters are shared across time.
  • 5. Calculating Sequence Loss
    Type a sentence and watch the engine calculate the error (loss) at every single word prediction to create a total score.
  • 6. Word Embeddings Pipeline
    Neural networks can't read the word "apple". Type a sentence and see how it gets translated into dense numerical vectors (heatmaps).
  • 7. BPTT & Gradient Clipping
    Watch the math flow backwards in a 3-phase animation. See how numbers multiply too fast (exploding gradients) and how "clipping" saves the network.
  • 8. Inside the LSTM Cell
    Click the Forget, Input, and Output gates to see how LSTMs act like a smart conveyor belt, selectively keeping or deleting memory to fix the RNN gradient problem.
  • 9. Architecture Evaluator
    Compare how RNNs, Dense Networks, and Attention mechanisms handle continuous streams, parallel processing, and long-term memory.
  • 10. Interactive Self-Attention
    Hover your mouse over words in different sentences. Watch glowing lines reveal exactly which other words the AI "looks at" to determine context.
  • 11. Query, Key, Value Simulator
    Based on a database search analogy. Watch the target word (Query) check every other word (Key) to extract mathematical meaning (Value).
  • 12. The Attention Chessboard
    Hover over a grid to see the exact Linear Algebra (Dot Product + Softmax) happening behind the scenes for any pair of words.
  • 13. Vision Transformer (Multi-Head)
    See how multiple attention heads pull apart a complex image. Click through the heads to see the AI isolate living entities, infrastructure, and nature independently.
"Architecture is about trade-offs. If you trade performance for developer experience, make sure the user can afford the bill."

By walking through these visualizations, the abstract math becomes concrete architecture. Click through the tools above, and demystify the engine powering today's AI revolution!
