System Protocol: Task-Based Delegation

01. Autocomplete to Delegation

In 2023, coding agents were glorified Autocomplete tools—they just finished your line of code. Today, we have entered the era of Task-based Delegation. Modern agents like Codex work on Flash Goals, allowing them to pursue a specific objective autonomously for over 100 hours without constant human monitoring.

02. The Three Pillars of Success

Context: Giving the Agent a "Workspace"

Instead of hand-crafting prompts (Context Engineering), we now use Context Gathering . You simply give the agent access to your "workspace"—Google Drive, Slack, and your screen. Tools like Chronicle record screen content to generate "memories," allowing the agent to find a "needle in a haystack" across deeply nested folders independently.

Validation: The Automated Self-Check

An agent shouldn't just do work; it must ensure it is correct. We equip agents with Linters—tools that ensure output conforms to specific standards . For example, the linter Vail ensures technical content follows a specific brand voice . Advanced validation even includes Computer Use, where the agent takes over a cursor to manually verify tasks in a web browser.

Verification: The Human Review

You shouldn't have to read every line of code to know the AI did well. The agent provides visual proof—like screenshots or preview links—so you can approve work from your phone . This often involves Multi-Agent Review, where a second agent reviews the first agent's work against updated guidelines.

The Climax: The Weekend Promotion

On a Friday, Dom told Codex to handle a marketing promotion deadline of 12:01 AM Monday . The agent scheduled its own work, monitored Slack for strategy changes, and verified human code approvals over the weekend . While Dom was away from his computer, the agent replied to colleagues on Slack and automatically deployed the changes at exactly midnight .
