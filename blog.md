# Blog — Feeling the Weights

> Field notes on building with artificial intelligence — pragmatic notes on AI fundamentals, tooling evaluations, and probabilistic system design.

## Overview

The RawWeights blog is a log of building with AI: what works, what breaks, what the math actually means in practice. Articles cover AI fundamentals with interactive tools, conference and meetup takeaways, build-in-public project logs, and deep dives on specific AI systems and tools. The blog is searchable and filterable by category.

The hero headline — "Feeling the Weights: Neural Networks Meet the Real World" — captures the editorial stance: this is practitioner writing, not academic survey writing.

## Key Topics / Sections

- **Latest Articles**: Dynamically loaded article grid from the site content data, showing the most recent posts with tags, dates, and preview text.
- **LinkedIn Carousel**: Selected LinkedIn posts from the author covering AI news, quick takes, and in-the-moment reactions to AI developments.
- **Category Filter**: Articles organised by topic — Fundamentals, Agentic AI, Computer Vision, NLP/LLMs, Tools & Stack, Build-in-Public.
- **Search**: Full-text search across article titles and descriptions.

## Article Categories Covered

- **Agentic AI**: Architecture strategy, best practices, conference takeaways, coding agents, voice AI
- **Fundamentals & Playgrounds**: ML theory/intuition, RNNs to Transformers, CNN vision, generative models, reinforcement learning
- **LLM Post-Training**: Fine-tuning guides, RLVR/GRPO, custom assistants
- **Build-in-Public**: ThreatScope analytics, BioLogic OSINT, Java ML experiments
- **Performance & Engineering**: Core Web Vitals, server monitoring
